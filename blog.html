A Closer Look: The Zari Weave of Banaras

By Ananya Varma • 7 Min Deep Read

For centuries, the ghaths and historic alleys of Banaras have echoed the rhythmic clatter of pit looms. Each weave is a testament to mindful slowness: real silver wires electroplated with 24-karat gold passing through lustrous mulberry silk warps, crafting heirlooms destined to be treasured across generations.

When styling such heirloom textiles, let the metallic luster take center stage. Pair with an understated boat-neck blouse and minimal kundan accents to achieve an unforgettable balance of heritage poise and understated luxury.
